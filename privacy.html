Who we are

Kitty Corral: Block Puzzle (“the game”, called 围住小猫 in Chinese) is made by Whisker Works Studio, an independent developer. This policy explains what information the game uses and why. If you have questions, email whiskerworksstudioai@gmail.com.

The short version

  • There are no accounts. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or location.
  • Your progress (levels, stars, cats, items, settings) is stored only on your device.
  • The game shows ads through Google AdMob, which uses your device's advertising ID and similar data to show and measure ads.
  • We may use Google Firebase Analytics to see, in aggregate, how the game is played (for example which levels are too hard).

Information stored on your device

Game progress, settings, your daily check-in streak and simple play counters are saved on your phone (in the app's local storage). They are not sent to us. Deleting the app deletes them.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game offers optional rewarded video ads. To serve them, Google AdMob may collect and process: your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID or Apple IDFA, only if you allow tracking), IP address, device and operating system information, and how you interact with ads. Google may use this information to show personalised or non-personalised ads, to measure ad performance and to prevent fraud.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time. On iPhone, personalised ads are only used if you allow tracking. You can also reset or limit your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

See how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Analytics (Google Firebase)

To improve the game, we may use Google Firebase Analytics. It records gameplay events such as level started, level won or lost, items used and ads watched, together with an app-instance identifier, device model, operating system, country and app version. We only look at this information in aggregate; it does not include your name or contact details. See firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the reminder is scheduled on your device. No notification data is sent to us.

Sharing your results

When you tap “Share”, the game creates a picture of your result and opens your phone's share sheet. Nothing is shared unless you choose an app to share it with.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Data retention and your rights

We do not keep personal information on our own servers. Data processed by Google is kept according to Google's policies; analytics data is kept for at most 14 months. You may contact us to ask about, access or delete data related to your use of the game, and you may opt out of personalised ads as described above.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.

简要说明

  • 本游戏没有账号，不会要求你提供姓名、邮箱、电话、通讯录、照片或位置。
  • 你的游戏进度（关卡、星星、猫咪、道具、设置）只保存在你自己的手机上。

  • 我们可能使用 Google Firebase Analytics，以汇总的方式了解玩家怎么玩（例如哪些关卡太难）。

保存在你设备上的信息

游戏进度、设置、每日打卡记录和简单的游玩次数统计保存在你的手机本地，不会发送给我们。删除 App 后这些数据也会一起删除。

广告（Google AdMob）

本游戏提供可自愿观看的激励视频广告。为了投放广告，Google AdMob 可能收集和处理：设备的广告标识符 （安卓广告 ID；iPhone 上仅在你允许跟踪时使用 IDFA）、IP 地址、设备和系统信息，以及你和广告的互动。 Google 可能用这些信息投放个性化或非个性化广告、统计广告效果以及防止作弊。

如果你在欧洲经济区、英国或瑞士，本游戏会在显示个性化广告前征求你的同意，你可以随时更改选择。 在 iPhone 上，只有在你允许跟踪时才会使用个性化广告。你也可以在手机设置里重置或限制广告标识符。

Google 如何使用数据：policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

数据统计（Google Firebase）

为了改进游戏，我们可能使用 Google Firebase Analytics。它会记录游戏事件（例如开始关卡、过关或失败、使用道具、观看广告）， 以及 App 实例标识符、设备型号、操作系统、国家和 App 版本。我们只查看汇总后的数据，其中不包括你的姓名或联系方式。 详见 firebase.google.com/support/privacy。

通知

如果你打开每日提醒，提醒只在你的手机上排程，不会向我们发送任何通知数据。

分享战绩

点“分享战绩”时，游戏会生成一张战绩图片并打开手机的分享面板。除非你选择了要分享到的 App，否则不会分享任何内容。

儿童

本游戏不面向 13 岁以下的儿童，我们不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。如果你认为有儿童提供了个人信息，请联系我们，我们会协助删除。

数据保留和你的权利

我们不在自己的服务器上保存个人信息。由 Google 处理的数据按照 Google 的政策保存；统计数据最多保留 14 个月。 你可以联系我们查询、获取或删除与你使用本游戏有关的数据，也可以按上文所述关闭个性化广告。

政策变更

如果本政策有变更，我们会更新本页面和顶部的日期。
